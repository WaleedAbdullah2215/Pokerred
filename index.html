    How to play

    Build your best five-card hand from your two private cards and the five cards shared by the table. Win by having the strongest hand at showdown or being the last player left in the hand.

    Check when the action costs zero chips. Call to match the current bet, raise to increase it, or fold to sit out the rest of the hand. The board is dealt in three stages after the opening round: flop, turn, and river.
